Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how One Task ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to identify your account, whether you sign up with Email + Password or with "Continue with Google."
  • Content you create in the app — your tasks, subtasks, journal entries, tags, and related settings, so the app can save and sync them for you.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone data, browsing history, or any information unrelated to the app's core functionality.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • Save your tasks and journal entries, and keep them synced automatically across your devices.
  • Let you use the app while offline, syncing your changes once you're back online.

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell or share it with third parties for marketing purposes. One Task does not display ads.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your account and content are stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore database), which runs on Google Cloud infrastructure. A copy of your data is also stored locally on your device so the app works offline.

Data Security

Your data is protected by server-side security rules that only allow your own signed-in account to read or modify your data — no other user of the app can access it.

Third-Party Services

One Task uses Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. Google's handling of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. If you sign in with Google, Google's own privacy practices also apply to that sign-in process. We do not use any advertising or analytics/tracking services.

Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly within the app:

Open the menu (☰) → Settings → Delete Account

This immediately and permanently deletes your account and all of your cloud-stored tasks, journal entries, and tags. This action cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

One Task is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
